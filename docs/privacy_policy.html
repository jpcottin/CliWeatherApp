Privacy Policy – WeatherGlance

Last updated: May 20, 2026

WeatherGlance ("the app") is a weather application developed by Jean-Philippe Cottin. This policy explains what information the app collects, how it is used, and your rights.

1. Information Collected

WeatherGlance collects only the minimum information required to display weather data:

  • Location (optional): If you enable GPS mode, the app requests your device location to automatically fetch local weather. Location is used solely to form the weather API request and is never stored on any server.
  • Manually entered coordinates: If you tap a location on the map, those coordinates are used in the same way and stored locally on your device only.

The app does not collect names, email addresses, device identifiers, usage analytics, crash reports, or any other personal data.

2. How Information Is Used

Your location coordinates (latitude and longitude) are sent to the following third-party weather services to retrieve forecast data:

  • Open-Meteo – a free, open-source weather API. No account or API key is required. Coordinates are sent as anonymous query parameters. See their terms of service.
  • Android Geocoder – used to reverse-geocode coordinates into a human-readable city name. This may use Google's servers depending on your device.

No other use is made of your location data.

3. Local Storage

The app stores the following data locally on your device using Android SharedPreferences:

  • Temperature unit preference (°C / °F)
  • Language preference
  • Time format preference (12h / 24h)
  • Last known coordinates (to reload weather on next launch)
  • Display settings (hourly range, UV index, air quality toggles)

This data never leaves your device and is deleted when you uninstall the app.

4. Third-Party Services

WeatherGlance uses Google Play Services to obtain GPS location on your device. Google's privacy policy applies to that component: https://policies.google.com/privacy.

5. Children's Privacy

WeatherGlance does not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. The app contains no advertising, in-app purchases, or user accounts.

6. Changes to This Policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted at this URL with a revised "Last updated" date.
